ABOUT METER SAFETY

Know Before You Ride

Building a community where passengers can share information and make informed decisions about their rides.

OUR PURPOSE

Safety information should be accessible.

Meter Safety is a community-focused platform designed to help passengers share and find information about ride experiences.

The platform is intended for people using ride-hailing services such as Uber and Bolt who want to make informed decisions before entering a vehicle.

How Meter Safety Works

01

Passengers Report

Users can submit information about their ride experience and provide relevant details.

02

Information Is Reviewed

Reports can be reviewed before information is made publicly available.

03

Passengers Search

Other users can search by driver, vehicle or location.

Our Principles

Accuracy

Information should be submitted honestly and based on genuine experiences.

Responsibility

Users should avoid submitting information that is misleading, abusive or unnecessary.

Privacy

The platform should collect and display only information necessary for its safety purpose.

Community

Meter Safety is designed around passengers helping other passengers stay informed.

THE FUTURE

Growing beyond a website.

Meter Safety is designed with the possibility of expanding into a larger safety platform, including stronger verification systems, mobile applications, location-based services, moderation tools and potential partnerships with ride-hailing platforms.
